# Sources

## Acadie 1: the winter on Sainte-Croix

Everything in `src/units/acadie-1/sources.js` comes from `../acadie/src/sources.js`, which was checked against the
originals on 2026-11-19 (see `../acadie/docs/SOURCES.md` for every judgment call).

| Source | What we use | Rights |
|---|---|---|
| Samuel de Champlain, *Les Voyages du sieur de Champlain* (Paris, 1613) | page scans (McGill University Library, Lande collection, via the Internet Archive), the French as printed by Laverdière (1870, Gutenberg #17258), the English of C. P. Otis (1878, Gutenberg #6749) | public domain |
| U.S. National Park Service, Saint Croix Island IHS pages | quotes and shortened versions | public domain (U.S. government) |
| Parks Canada, Saint Croix Island NHS pages | quotes and shortened versions | © Parks Canada, quoted for classroom use |

"Today's words" are Acadie's plain retellings, written from each passage alone. Nothing is added.

Changes made here (2026-11-25):

- Each source got a label (`meta`). Champlain's says he "sailed with de Mons in 1604" (his own pages and the NPS
  pages both place him on the expedition and on the island). The NPS pages are dated "2021 or later" and the Parks
  Canada pages "2022 to 2024", as Acadie dates them.
- The Parks Canada "Winter of Despair" retelling now quotes de Mons's sworn statement with its ellipses, as Parks
  Canada prints it, instead of paraphrasing it, so students can point at the primary source inside it.
- Shift 4 says Champlain's French calls the people "sauvages" and today's words don't: true of the French text and the
  retellings in this unit.
